my story

People are the heart of what I do — the homes are just the perk.

As a Tulsa native, I’m deeply connected to this community and the people who make it special. In 2025 I’ve been honored to help 37 — and counting — families and individuals buy or sell their homes.

Whether I’m helping a family transform their space to sell or guiding buyers as they imagine their life in a new home, my goal is always to make the process personal, meaningful, and rooted in care.

Every closing represents someone’s new beginning — and that’s what fuels me every single day. If you’re ready for your next chapter, I’d love to help you find the perfect space to start it.
